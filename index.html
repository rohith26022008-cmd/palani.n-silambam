About N Palani

Life of a Silambam Master

N Palani is a traditional Silambam Aasaan from Ramapuram, Chennai, Tamil Nadu. His life reflects a deep connection with Silambam, one of the traditional martial arts of Tamil culture.

Through years of practice and teaching, he has continued to share Silambam knowledge with younger generations, emphasizing discipline, dedication and respect for tradition.

Alongside his Silambam journey, N Palani serves as Aalaya Dharma Kartha at Shri Muthumariyamman Temple, Vaikundapuram, Nungambakkam, Chennai. This responsibility reflects his involvement in temple and community life.

A tradition remains alive when its knowledge is passed from one generation to the next.
