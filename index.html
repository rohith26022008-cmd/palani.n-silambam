About the Aasaan

Life of a Silambam Master

Palani N is a Silambam Aasaan from Ramapuram, Tamil Nadu, with more than 50 years of experience in the traditional Tamil martial art of Silambam.

His journey reflects a long-standing commitment to practicing and sharing the knowledge of this traditional martial art.

Through his role as an Aasaan, he represents the importance of traditional knowledge, discipline and the continuation of Silambam.

A tradition remains alive when its knowledge is passed from one generation to the next.
His Journey

Five Decades of Dedication

With over 50 years of experience, Palani N has developed extensive familiarity with Silambam and its traditional practices.

His journey as a Silambam Aasaan highlights the value of experience, discipline, teaching and cultural knowledge.

This official website presents his story, photographs and contributions for people interested in learning about his journey.
